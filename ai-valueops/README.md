# AI ValueOps: From AI Experiments to Business Outcomes

**Type:** Independent product-management case study  
**Focus:** AI adoption · Product strategy · Portfolio prioritisation · Adoption analytics · ROI measurement · Governance

> **Transparency:** This is a hypothetical portfolio exercise inspired by a general 2026 business challenge. It is not a client deployment and reports no real company outcomes. Any example values are illustrative assumptions.

## Problem

Organisations may run many AI pilots without clear ownership, validated baselines, workflow integration, user adoption plans, or finance-approved value measurement. Pilot activity is not the same as business impact.

**Product question:** How might leaders decide which AI initiatives to fund, how to help teams adopt them, and when to scale, improve, or stop each initiative?

## Product concept

AI ValueOps is a decision-support platform for an AI initiative portfolio.

- **Prioritise:** Compare initiatives by business impact, feasibility, data readiness, adoption effort, risk, and measurement confidence.
- **Assign ownership:** Require a business owner, sponsor, baseline, target, and decision date for each pilot.
- **Track adoption:** Monitor eligible users, repeat usage, workflow completion, quality, overrides, and support burden.
- **Validate value:** Separate projected benefits, pilot-observed effects, and finance-validated realised value.
- **Govern decisions:** Record evidence and rationale for scale, iterate, pause, or stop decisions.

## Measurement framework

- **Adoption:** eligible users activated, repeat usage, workflow completion.
- **Efficiency:** cycle time, handling time, rework, cost per case.
- **Quality:** acceptance/correction rate, error severity, user satisfaction.
- **Economics:** validated benefits less implementation, model, support, and risk costs.
- **Guardrails:** privacy/security incidents, harmful errors, policy violations, complaints, and human-override rate.

## Interview framing

Present this as an independent product concept. Explain how you would start with one measurable workflow, establish a baseline, involve Finance and Risk early, and prove value before scaling. Do not describe the scenario as deployed work or claim unmeasured ROI.
