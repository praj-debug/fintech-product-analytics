# Product Brief: AI ValueOps

## Problem
Leaders struggle to compare AI initiatives and verify value when pilots lack clear ownership, baselines, adoption plans, and consistent measures.

## Target users
- Business leaders deciding where to invest.
- Workflow owners accountable for operational outcomes.
- Product and AI teams delivering pilots.
- Finance, Risk, Security, and Legal stakeholders validating value and controls.

## Goal
Help organisations make evidence-based scale, iterate, pause, or stop decisions for AI initiatives.

## User story
As a business leader, I want to compare AI initiatives using consistent evidence about impact, feasibility, adoption, risk, and realised value so I can direct investment toward outcomes rather than demos.

## P0 requirements
- Register initiative, problem statement, workflow, target users, owner, sponsor, baseline, and decision date.
- Record hypotheses, success criteria, risk review, data readiness, and dependencies.
- Provide a transparent prioritisation rubric with visible assumptions.
- Track pilot stage, eligible users, adoption, workflow completion, quality, cost, and incidents.
- Separate projected value, observed pilot effect, and finance-validated realised value.
- Capture evidence and rationale for scale, iterate, pause, or stop decisions.

## P1 requirements
- Compare cohorts or pilot/control groups where appropriate.
- Provide alerts for adoption stalls, quality degradation, cost overruns, and risk events.
- Export a decision-ready portfolio review for leadership.

## Non-goals
- Automatically approving high-risk AI deployments.
- Treating model usage or number of demos as proof of business value.
- Claiming time saved as cash saved without a credible conversion mechanism.
- Allowing AI to bypass security, privacy, compliance, or human-review requirements.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every active pilot has a named business owner and measurable baseline.
- Success criteria and guardrails are agreed before pilot launch.
- Adoption and quality can be reviewed alongside costs and incidents.
- Financial assumptions are visible and can be validated by Finance.
- Each initiative has a recorded decision and rationale at its review date.
