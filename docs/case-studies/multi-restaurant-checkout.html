Problem

When a food-ordering product supports multiple restaurant baskets or related checkout flows, users need to understand which items belong to which restaurant, how fees and delivery timing work, and what happens when one part of the order changes.

Review framework

Discoverability

Can users find the feature and understand when it applies?

Cost clarity

Are fees, minimums and delivery charges visible before confirmation?

Cart comprehension

Are restaurant boundaries and item groupings obvious?

Recovery

Can users recover when a restaurant or item becomes unavailable?

Product recommendations

  • Label each restaurant basket clearly throughout checkout.
  • Explain the combined total and any separate delivery charges.
  • Show separate ETA expectations where relevant.
  • Design clear recovery when one basket cannot proceed.

Prajakta’s product takeaway

Checkout clarity is a trust and conversion feature. When a basket spans multiple restaurants or fulfilment rules, users need to understand what is grouped, what costs extra and what happens after payment. Clear grouping and transparent totals should be tested against checkout completion, errors and support contacts.
