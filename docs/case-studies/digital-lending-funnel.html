Digital Lending Funnel Optimization

Identify where lending applications lose volume and connect the funnel signals to operational actions.

View full project on GitHub ↗Print / Save as PDF

Problem

A lending journey can look healthy at the lead stage while losing value between approval and disbursement. End-to-end conversion alone hides where the friction occurs.

Scenario findings

Synthetic dataset

500 applications

152 approvals (30.4%) and 89 disbursements (17.8%).

Operational signal

54.4% SLA breaches

272 of 500 example cases breach SLA, making exception management a priority.

Only 58.6% of approved applications reach disbursement in this example dataset. This points to a post-approval leakage problem worth investigating alongside SLA hotspots.

Recommended approach

  1. Segment conversion by partner, channel, product and city.
  2. Compare P50, P75 and P90 turnaround time rather than relying on averages.
  3. Create an approved-but-not-disbursed recovery queue with an owner and next action.
  4. Prioritise recurring SLA and API/UAT exceptions.

Measurement plan

Approval → disbursement conversionP75/P90 TATSLA breach rateException backlog

Guardrails include approval quality, API issue rate and operational workload.

Prajakta’s product takeaway

A funnel is useful only when it reveals an actionable bottleneck. Stage conversion, ageing and SLA breaches should be read together to identify where users get stuck and why. Prioritising the largest fixable drop-off gives Product and Operations a measurable path to improve completion.
