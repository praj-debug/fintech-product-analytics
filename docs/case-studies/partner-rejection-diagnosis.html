Diagnosing Partner-Stage Rejections

A product and lending-operations framework for understanding why platform-approved applications may be rejected by lending partners.

Print / Save as PDFView project on GitHub ↗

Problem

A lending marketplace can show strong early engagement while losing applicants between platform approval-in-principle and a partner's final underwriting decision. The key question is whether the platform's eligibility signals align with each partner's underwriting policy.

Working hypothesis

Partner-stage rejection may reflect differences between platform-visible signals and proprietary partner scorecards, as well as data-quality gaps, journey friction and sourcing quality. The analysis should distinguish what the platform can influence from what depends on partner-only risk signals.

Diagnosis approach

Partner and cohort cuts

  • Compare rejection by partner, bureau band, employment type and income band.
  • Separate self-service and assisted journeys.
  • Review channel and sourcing patterns.

Validate before changing policy

  • Request structured rejection reasons and available score bands.
  • Check declared versus verified data where permitted.
  • Separate scorecard mismatch from addressable process friction.

Recommended actions

  1. Build a partner-level rejection MIS with consistent reason categories.
  2. Identify concentrated failure cohorts rather than relying on a single overall rejection rate.
  3. Introduce pre-submission checks for addressable data-quality issues.
  4. Review findings with Product, Risk, Operations and lending partners.
  5. Track rejection mix and downstream disbursement after any change.

Success measures

Partner-stage approval rateRejection reason mixApproval-to-disbursal conversionDocument completionPartner decision TAT

Guardrails should include credit quality and fair, policy-compliant treatment. A lower rejection rate alone is not success if it degrades underwriting quality.

Data note: This is an independent portfolio analysis. Funnel volumes and scenario outcomes in supporting files are synthetic and are not claimed as production performance or confidential partner data.
