StockRani: Everyday Personal Finance

A personal-finance education concept spanning budgeting, saving, credit, debt, banking, investing and financial decision-making.

View full project on GitHub ↗Print / Save as PDF

Purpose

StockRani is intended to make everyday money decisions easier to understand, especially for beginners and young professionals. It covers more than stock-market investing.

Content pillars

Manage money

Budgeting, saving, expense planning and emergency funds.

Understand credit

Credit scores, loans, EMIs, debt and repayment.

Navigate finance

Banking, cards, interest rates and financial products.

Build long-term security

Investing basics, insurance, risk and wealth habits.

Editorial principles

  • Use plain language and practical examples.
  • Explain costs, risks and trade-offs, not just benefits.
  • Separate education from personalised financial advice.
  • Keep claims and product information current and sourced.
