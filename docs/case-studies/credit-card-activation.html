01 · Scope and ownership

Operating across the full partner and customer lifecycle

My responsibility covered the operational execution of personal-loan and credit-card products from launch readiness and partner onboarding through customer servicing and ongoing performance tracking. I worked with sales teams, account managers and partner stakeholders to keep the customer journey and partner processes connected.

Partner and launch operations

  • Coordinated product launches and partner onboarding for PL and CC journeys, growing the PL lender network from 5 to approximately 15 partners and onboarding around 10 CC partners.
  • Worked with partner and internal teams to align operational steps, data requirements and handoffs.
  • Tracked partner dependencies and ongoing changes that affected customer eligibility, routing or activation.
  • Supported customer journeys with sales teams and account managers, helping move cases through the relevant product and partner process.

Product and customer journey operations

  • Tracked changes to the Business Rules Engine (BRE), including approval-criteria changes and their operational impact.
  • Monitored customer activation journeys and MoEngage activation activity, following customer progress and campaign-driven activation outcomes.
  • Supported the move from a web application journey to an app-only journey; app downloads increased during the transition.
  • Maintained visibility of personal-loan and credit-card application outcomes.
  • Coordinated cross-product routing when an application could not proceed through its original product path.
02 · Product journey

Connecting the PL and CC journeys instead of treating them as silos

A customer who could not proceed through one product journey could potentially be served through the other, subject to the applicable eligibility and partner rules. I tracked these outcomes and handoffs, including PL-to-CC and CC-to-PL cross-sell, so an unsuccessful product path did not automatically become a dead end. For rejected PL leads, I also worked through DSA networks to identify lenders with lower-CIBIL eligibility appetite and helped build a rerouting model that sent suitable leads to alternate partners for possible disbursal and commission revenue.

Routing remained subject to each lender’s eligibility rules; it did not mean every rejected applicant was approved.

03 · Activation and rule changes

Keeping product changes connected to customer activation

I tracked changes in BRE and monitored activation activity through MoEngage so that partner, product and customer-facing teams had visibility into changes affecting the journey. This connected configuration changes and activation execution with the cases moving through the operating funnel. I also worked with the BRE team on approval-criteria changes, using lead-quality observations and partner feedback to support adjustments in the operating funnel.

What I monitored and coordinated

  • BRE changes and their effect on the applicable PL or CC journey.
  • MoEngage activation activity and customer follow-through, including monitoring activation across the PL and CC journeys.
  • Application status and product outcome across PL and CC.
  • Cases requiring a change in route, a partner handoff or additional customer support.
  • Operational follow-ups with sales, account managers and partner teams.
04 · MIS and partner data exchange

Making partner performance and case movement visible

I prepared and shared MIS with partner stakeholders, received partner data and coordinated the exchange through the SFTP process. This supported a recurring operating loop between internal teams and partners: share the required information, receive partner-side updates, reconcile case movement and follow up on exceptions.

05 · Commercial and performance lens

Connecting conversion, CAC and revenue quality

I tracked conversion and customer movement alongside customer acquisition cost (CAC) and commercial performance, using MIS and partner data to understand which product and partner paths were contributing stronger business value. I ran performance-based lead-allocation experiments across 12 partners and used partner-level conversion and revenue performance to help prioritise acquisition spend.

Lead-volume scale: Daily inbound lead volume grew from approximately 1,000 to 10,000 leads per day (about 10×). The share of inbound leads processed increased from 20% to 80%. I tracked lead quality and partner-level outcomes as part of the operating funnel.

PL disbursal scale: Monthly personal-loan disbursals grew from ₹1 Cr+ to ₹15 Cr+. My contribution included analysing lead quality by DSA, working with the BRE team on approval-criteria relaxation, sharing credit-policy guidance with DSAs to improve lead fit, and expanding the network with additional DSAs and STPL lenders such as Ram Fincorp.

Commercial context: The business reached approximately ₹1 Cr in transactions per day during the period. The DSA channel contributed 2.5% of overall company revenue across a managed portfolio of 385+ DSAs.

Performance dimensions

  • Product-wise and partner-wise conversion, including approval-to-disbursal conversion improvement of 15–18% through SQL and Metabase funnel analysis.
  • Movement between PL and CC journeys and the outcomes of eligible rerouted leads.
  • Customer activation and follow-through monitored through MoEngage.
  • CAC in relation to conversion outcomes and partner-level acquisition efficiency; no CAC reduction percentage is claimed.
  • Revenue contribution and identification of stronger-performing paths, including performance-based lead allocation across 12 partners.

Decision support

  • Shared daily MIS to keep teams aligned on product and partner performance.
  • Used received partner data through SFTP to maintain a view of case progress and outcomes.
  • Tracked changes and exceptions that could affect conversion or customer experience.
  • Reviewed CAC against conversion and prioritised higher-revenue partner paths; no unsupported CAC saving is stated.

The 15–18% conversion improvement and aggregate business figures above are included as period-level outcomes. CAC was tracked against conversion and revenue quality, but no CAC reduction percentage or absolute CAC value is published because a verified, attributable figure is not available for this write-up. The ₹1 Cr/day transaction figure is presented as overall business context, not assigned to PL or CC individually.

06 · Cross-functional execution

Keeping the operating system joined up

The work sat between product configuration, partner operations, sales execution and customer servicing. I coordinated with sales teams and account managers for customer follow-through, and with partner stakeholders for onboarding, data exchange, reporting and application outcomes. The practical focus was to keep ownership and next steps visible across handoffs.

Capabilities demonstrated

  • 0→1 product launch and partner onboarding execution.
  • Customer journey operations across two lending products.
  • BRE change tracking and activation monitoring through MoEngage.
  • Cross-product routing oversight, including alternate-lender rerouting and PL ↔ CC cross-sell follow-up.
  • MIS preparation, partner reporting and SFTP-based data coordination.
  • SQL and Metabase funnel analysis, with 15–18% approval-to-disbursal conversion improvement.
  • CAC, acquisition efficiency and revenue-oriented partner performance tracking.
What I learned

Product operations is where the product promise meets execution

Launching a financial product is not just a go-live milestone. The product has to work across partner processes, business rules, customer communication, sales support, data exchange and ongoing performance monitoring. My work across PL and CC gave me practical experience connecting those moving parts and keeping the customer journey moving when one route did not work.
