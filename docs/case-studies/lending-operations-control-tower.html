Problem

Manual case-by-case follow-up makes it hard to spot repeatable partner, process or technology issues. The team needs a decision surface that shows which exceptions need attention, who owns them and what happens next.

Operating model

Detect → Diagnose → Assign → Resolve → Measure

Every exception should have an owner, next action, target resolution time, root-cause category and closure outcome. Recurring patterns should feed back into product, partner or engineering priorities.

Example portfolio signals

Funnel health

  • Approval rate: 30.4%
  • End-to-end disbursement rate: 17.8%

Operations health

  • SLA breach rate: 54.4%
  • Track P75/P90 turnaround time.
  • Surface aged exceptions and API-related issues.

Exception prioritisation

  • P0: API issue combined with an SLA breach, escalate to Tech and Operations.
  • P1: SLA breached or active integration issue, reprioritise and assign an owner.
  • P2: UAT or readiness dependency pending, follow up with a target date.
