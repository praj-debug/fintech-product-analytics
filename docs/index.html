StockRani was started by Prajakta Mane.

StockRani is an evolving financial-literacy and stock-market education initiative focused on making money concepts easier to understand. This page documents the thinking, decisions, operating approach and learning roadmap behind the project.

Part of a broader brand vision

StockRani is the financial-education property within the wider RupeeRani brand vision. StockRani Studio is intended for career and professional resources, while StockRani Decodes is an explanatory content format for companies, industries and business models. These are intended brand roles, not claims that each property has been independently monetized or validated.
