The problem

Complex journeys need more than another spreadsheet.

Education lending spans students, co-applicants, counsellors, operations teams and lender partners. When documents, decisions and follow-ups live in disconnected channels, ownership and next steps become difficult to see.

01 · VISIBILITY

Fragmented case context

Student details, academic records, lender references and existing-loan information are scattered across tools and conversations.

02 · OPERATIONS

Document and TAT friction

Different lender requirements, repeated submissions, expiry dates and unresolved queries create avoidable operational work.

03 · ACCOUNTABILITY

Unclear next action

Without clear ownership, task history and SLA visibility, cases can stall between student, team and lender.

Two distinct journeys

One workspace. Separate lending workflows.

New education loans and balance transfers share core student and lender records, but keep their own stages, rules, checklists and operational metrics.

Role-aware product design

Different teams. Relevant workspaces.

Tranche is designed for all participants, with shared context and permission boundaries rather than one overloaded interface.

Relationship Managers

Assigned cases, follow-ups, counselling and student communication.

Loan Operations

Document verification, lender logins, pending queries and disbursement tasks.

Partner Managers

Lender products, eligibility criteria, application outcomes and TAT.

Management

Business overview, funnel health, lender scorecards and operational risks.

Students

Application status, document requests, secure uploads and messages.

Lender Representatives

Assigned lender applications and explicitly permitted information only.

Core product capabilities

Designed around the operating details.

These are the product capabilities and prototype scope, not claims that every integration is live.

01 · STUDENT 360

Shared borrower context

Student, co-applicant, academic plan, financial profile, existing loan and linked cases in one place.

02 · LENDER MANAGEMENT

Independent lender applications

Multiple lender applications per case, product-specific criteria, offer comparison and decision history.

03 · DOCUMENT OPERATIONS

One document vault

Student self-upload and internal uploads, dynamic checklists, verification, resubmissions and version history.

04 · DISBURSEMENT

Tranche-level tracking

Sanction conditions, agreement steps, partial disbursements and final disbursement status.

05 · OMNICHANNEL

One communication timeline

WhatsApp, email, SMS and student-portal interactions represented in one auditable timeline. Prototype messages are simulated.

06 · CONTROL & INSIGHT

Tasks, SLA and analytics

Hybrid assignment, follow-ups, SLA pause/resume, case ageing, lender scorecards and rejection analysis.

Product principles

Useful before impressive.

Clear navigation, one obvious next action, commission-blind lender recommendations, consent-aware document sharing and audit history by design. Every public demo record is synthetic.
