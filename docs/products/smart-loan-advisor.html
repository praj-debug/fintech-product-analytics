Smart Loan Advisor

A multi-product loan decision-support prototype bringing profile assessment, repayment estimation and affordability context into one guided experience.

Open the interactive product ↗View product files ↗

I built this prototype to explore how a borrower could review an indicative lending assessment across different product types without navigating five disconnected calculators. One shared profile adapts to the selected product and presents an estimate with the factors behind it.

01 · Problem framing

The product problem

Loan discovery often makes users interpret eligibility, monthly repayment and affordability as separate questions. This prototype brings those questions into one flow, while making clear that an indicative estimate is not a lender decision or approval promise.

02 · Product scope

One experience across five lending products

Personal Loan (PL)

Loan amount, tenure, assumed interest rate, estimated EMI, total repayment and total interest.

Credit Card (CC)

Desired credit limit, existing total card limits and current utilisation, with an illustrative potential-limit estimate.

Home Loan (HL)

Loan amount, tenure, assumed rate and down payment, with EMI and repayment estimates.

Education Loan (EL)

Loan amount, tenure, assumed rate and optional co-applicant / parent income in the affordability assessment.

Car Loan

Loan amount, tenure, assumed rate and down payment, with estimated repayment and affordability context.

03 · User journey

How the experience works

1. Choose productSelect PL, CC, HL, EL or Car Loan. The form adapts to show relevant fields.
2. Enter profileProvide bureau score, monthly income, existing EMIs, employment details and product-specific inputs.
3. Review estimateSee an indicative assessment, repayment figures, FOIR and affordability headroom, with explanatory factors.
04 · Inputs and decision logic

What the prototype evaluates

Borrower profile

  • CIBIL score (300–900 input range)
  • Monthly net income
  • Existing monthly EMIs
  • Employment type and work / business vintage

Product-specific details

  • Requested amount or credit limit
  • Tenure and editable illustrative interest rate
  • Down payment for home and car loans
  • Co-applicant / parent income for education loans
  • Existing card limits and utilisation for credit cards

Indicative calculations

  • EMI using a reducing-balance formula for instalment loans
  • Total repayment and estimated total interest
  • FOIR after proposed EMI
  • Indicative affordable EMI headroom
  • Credit-card limit estimate using a simple income / existing-limit heuristic

Assessment explanation

  • Rule-based score bands for bureau score
  • Affordability bands using estimated FOIR
  • Employment / business vintage signals
  • Down-payment signal for secured lending
  • Card-utilisation signal for credit-card assessment
05 · Output design

The result is more than a number

Repayment view

Estimated monthly EMI, total repayment and total interest show both monthly and full-tenure implications.

Affordability view

FOIR after the proposed loan and indicative EMI headroom provide a simple view of obligations relative to income.

Assessment band

A directional status groups the prototype's rule-based signals into stronger indicative fit, needs lender review or potential eligibility concerns.

Reasoning cues

Explanatory bullets show which entered factors influenced the result, rather than presenting a score without context.

06 · Product safeguards

An educational screening prototype

Important boundary: This tool does not generate a credit score, approval probability, pre-approved offer or lender decision. Thresholds and calculations are illustrative assumptions, not lender policy. Actual underwriting can consider bureau history, income stability, bank statements, employer or business profile, product rules, geography, collateral, co-applicant strength and lender-specific criteria. The demo states that entered data is not transmitted or stored.
07 · Next iterations

What I would validate next

Validate assumptions

Review affordability thresholds, score bands and product heuristics with domain experts before any real-world use.

Improve explanations

Make each estimate traceable to the inputs and assumptions that produced it, with clearer product-specific guidance.

Test usability

Check whether users understand FOIR, headroom, assumed rates and the difference between an indicative result and a lender decision.

Expand product depth

Explore lender-specific policy configuration only where reliable, approved rules and governance are available.

08 · Build note

My role and the nature of this work

This is an independent product build and interactive prototype in my portfolio. It demonstrates product framing, multi-product form design, basic financial calculations, decision-support logic, explanatory UX and risk-aware product boundaries. It is not represented as a live lender integration or production underwriting system.

Assumptions and sample values are illustrative. No actual lender approval outcomes or performance metrics are claimed.
