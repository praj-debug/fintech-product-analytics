# Product Insights & Business Recommendations

## 1. Executive Summary

This project analyzes a synthetic digital lending portfolio to identify product, operational, customer journey, partner, and technology opportunities.

The analysis connects lending funnel performance with operational KPIs such as processing time, SLA adherence, approval, disbursal, API issues, channel performance, and partner performance.

The objective is to move from raw operational data to actionable product and business decisions.

---

## 2. Business Questions

The analysis focuses on the following questions:

1. Where are customers dropping out of the lending funnel?
2. Which partners generate the strongest business outcomes?
3. Which cities and channels perform better?
4. Where are SLA breaches concentrated?
5. Which lending products have stronger disbursal potential?
6. Are API or operational issues creating customer friction?
7. Which parts of the customer journey require product intervention?
8. Where can automation improve lending operations?
9. Which partner relationships should be prioritized?
10. What product changes could improve conversion and turnaround time?

# 5. Funnel Analysis

The lending funnel can be represented as:

**Lead → Application → Documents → Credit Review → Approved → Disbursed**

Each stage represents a potential customer or operational drop-off point.

### Product analytics approach

For every stage, evaluate:

- Number of applications
- Percentage of total applications
- Conversion to next stage
- Average processing time
- SLA performance
- Approval status
- Disbursal outcome

The objective is to identify the stages where intervention can create the highest business impact.

---

# 6. Operational Bottlenecks

Potential bottlenecks to investigate include:

### SLA Breaches

High processing times can indicate:

- Manual verification
- Operational queue buildup
- Partner-side delays
- Documentation issues
- Credit review delays
- Integration problems

### Product Response

Introduce an SLA monitoring layer that automatically identifies cases approaching breach.

Example:

**Green:** Within SLA  
**Amber:** Approaching SLA limit  
**Red:** SLA breached

This allows operations teams to prioritize exceptions instead of manually reviewing every application.

# 7. Partner Performance Framework

Partners can be segmented using four dimensions:

### High Volume + High Conversion

**Strategic Partners**

Actions:

- Increase business allocation
- Prioritize integration improvements
- Create joint growth plans

### High Volume + Low Conversion

**Optimization Partners**

Actions:

- Investigate lead quality
- Analyze customer drop-offs
- Improve funnel conversion

### Low Volume + High Conversion

**Growth Opportunity**

Actions:

- Increase lead generation
- Expand campaigns
- Improve partner engagement

### Low Volume + Low Conversion

**Low Priority**

Actions:

- Review economics
- Investigate operational issues
- Consider reducing allocation

# 8. Customer Journey Recommendations

## Recommendation 1: Application Status Tracker

Customers should be able to see:

**Application Received → Documents → Credit Review → Approved → Disbursal**

This reduces uncertainty and unnecessary customer support contacts.

---

## Recommendation 2: Proactive Notifications

Trigger notifications when:

- Documents are missing
- Application is under review
- Additional information is required
- Approval is completed
- Disbursal is initiated

---

## Recommendation 3: SLA-Based Prioritization

Applications approaching SLA breach should automatically move higher in the operations queue.

This creates a proactive rather than reactive operations model.

---

## Recommendation 4: Partner Performance Dashboard

Create a dashboard showing:

- Applications
- Approval rate
- Disbursal rate
- Loan value
- Average processing time
- SLA breach rate
- Revenue

This gives business teams a single view of partner performance.

---

## Recommendation 5: API Monitoring

For integrated lending journeys, track:

- API success rate
- API failures
- Retry rate
- Response time
- Partner-wise failure rate
- Customer impact

Technology failures should be treated as product metrics, not just engineering problems.
