# Experiment Plan

## Objective

Test whether proactive delay-risk communication improves the delayed-order experience without creating notification fatigue or unacceptable operating cost.

## Test design

### Control

Existing ETA and delay communication.

### Treatment

For eligible high-confidence delay-risk orders:

1. Trigger a proactive message.
2. Provide a revised ETA when confidence is sufficient.
3. Surface the applicable recovery path.

## Primary success signals

- Reduction in cancellation after ETA breach
- Reduction in support contacts for delayed orders

## Secondary signals

- ETA accuracy
- Within-ETA delivery rate
- Post-delay satisfaction
- Repeat ordering

## Guardrails

- Alert rate
- False-positive rate
- Refund / compensation cost
- Complaint rate
- Operational workload

## Follow-up segmentation

Review results by restaurant, geography, time of day, delay severity, order distance, and root-cause category.

## Rollout principle

Scale only when customer-friction metrics improve without material guardrail regression.
