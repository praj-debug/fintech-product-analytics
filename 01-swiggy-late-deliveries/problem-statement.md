# Problem Statement

## User problem

When a food order is likely to arrive late, the customer may receive little warning or repeated ETA changes. That creates uncertainty and can trigger cancellations, support contacts, and lower trust.

## Business problem

Late deliveries can increase service-recovery effort and create customer friction that may affect future ordering behavior.

## Opportunity

Detect meaningful delivery risk early enough to improve expectation-setting without overwhelming customers with alerts.

## Problem statement

> **How might we help customers feel informed and in control when an order is at risk of missing its communicated delivery time?**

## Assumptions to validate

1. A meaningful share of late deliveries is predictable before the ETA is breached.
2. Customers value accurate expectation-setting even when the revised ETA is later.
3. Proactive communication can reduce avoidable support contacts and cancellations.
