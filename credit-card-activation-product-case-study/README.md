# Fi Money: PL & CC Product Launch, Partner Integrations and Activation Operations

**Type:** Retrospective of professional experience at Fi Money  
**Focus:** Product operations · 0→1 launch execution · Partner onboarding · Customer activation · Cross-product routing · MIS · SFTP · Conversion and CAC

> This is real work I personally handled at Fi Money. It is not a hypothetical product exercise. The public write-up is sanitised and excludes customer-level information, partner-confidential data, internal screenshots, proprietary business rules, exact commercial terms and unverified performance figures.

## Overview

I handled operational execution across personal-loan (PL) and credit-card (CC) products from launch and partner onboarding through customer servicing and ongoing performance tracking. The work connected partner operations, product configuration, activation journeys, sales teams, account managers and customer follow-through.

## Scope of work

During the period, daily inbound lead volume grew from approximately **1,000 to 10,000 leads per day** (about 10×), and the share of inbound leads processed increased from **20% to 80%**. The PL lender network grew from **5 to approximately 15 partners**, and around **10 credit-card partners** were onboarded. These are aggregate business outcomes during the period; the contribution described below is the work I handled, not a claim of sole attribution.

### 1. Launch and partner onboarding
- Worked across PL and CC product launches and partner onboarding.
- Grew the PL lender network from 5 to approximately 15 partners and onboarded around 10 CC partners.
- Coordinated operational requirements, partner dependencies and internal handoffs.
- Supported customer journeys with sales teams and account managers.
- Tracked partner and product changes that affected the operating journey.

### 2. BRE and activation operations
- Kept track of changes to the Business Rules Engine (BRE), including approval-criteria changes and their impact on the product journey.
- Monitored customer activation activity and follow-through through MoEngage.
- Supported the move from web application journeys to an app-only journey; app downloads increased during the transition.
- Followed application outcomes and customer progress across both products.
- Coordinated operational follow-up when a case needed another route or partner action.

### 3. Cross-product routing: PL ↔ CC
- Tracked PL-to-CC and CC-to-PL cross-sell paths where an alternate product route might be available, subject to applicable rules and eligibility.
- When approximately 2,000 of 10,000 inbound leads were rejected, identified lower-CIBIL lenders through DSA networks and helped build a rerouting model to send suitable leads to alternate partners for possible disbursal and commission revenue.
- Tracked the reverse direction when a credit-card journey could not proceed and a personal-loan route was relevant.
- Maintained visibility of outcomes and handoffs rather than treating a failed product path as an automatic end to the customer journey.

This describes routing oversight and coordination. It does not imply every unsuccessful applicant was eligible for or approved through the alternate product.

### 4. MIS and SFTP partner data exchange
- Prepared and shared MIS with partner stakeholders.
- Received partner data through the agreed SFTP process.
- Coordinated case updates, operational reconciliation and follow-up on exceptions.

### 5. Conversion, CAC and revenue tracking
- Built SQL and Metabase funnel analysis to identify bottlenecks; approval-to-disbursal conversion improved by **15–18%**.
- Ran performance-based lead-allocation experiments across **12 partners** and used partner-level conversion and revenue performance to help prioritise acquisition spend.
- Tracked CAC against conversion and revenue contribution. No CAC reduction percentage or absolute CAC value is claimed because a verified, attributable figure is not available for this write-up.
- Monthly PL disbursals grew from **₹1 Cr+ to ₹15 Cr+** during the period. My contribution included analysing lead quality by DSA, working with the BRE team on approval-criteria relaxation, sharing credit-policy guidance with DSAs to improve lead fit, and expanding the network with additional DSAs and STPL lenders such as Ram Fincorp.
- The business reached approximately **₹1 Cr in transactions per day** during the period. The DSA channel contributed **2.5% of overall company revenue** across a managed portfolio of **385+ DSAs**.
- The ₹1 Cr/day transaction figure is presented as overall business context, not assigned to PL or CC individually.
