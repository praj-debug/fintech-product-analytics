## Portfolio Principles

- **Decision over dashboard:** every analysis should lead to an action.
- **Outcome over activity:** measure conversion, TAT, quality, customer impact, and business value.
- **Exceptions over averages:** averages hide the cases that hurt customers and operations.
- **Trade-offs matter:** recommendations should account for customer experience, risk, economics, and operational capacity.
- **Measure the change:** define primary, secondary, and guardrail metrics before rollout.
