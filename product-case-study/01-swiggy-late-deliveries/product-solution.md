# Product Solution

## Solution principle

**Detect early. Communicate clearly. Give the user control.**

### Delay-risk scoring

Use signals such as preparation-time variance, rider assignment / availability, distance, travel conditions, restaurant backlog, and historical on-time performance.

### Proactive message

Trigger only when risk crosses a confidence threshold.

Example:

> Your order may arrive about 15 minutes later than planned because the restaurant is taking longer than expected.

### Revised ETA

Show a revised ETA only when confidence is sufficient. Avoid repeatedly moving the timestamp.

### Recovery

Make the next action clear:

**Keep waiting | Get help | Cancel / applicable recovery path**

The exact cancellation and compensation rules would follow the product's operational policy.

### UX principle

Customers should not need to understand internal logistics. Translate operational complexity into a simple, honest expectation.
