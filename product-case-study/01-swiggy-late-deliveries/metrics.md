# Metrics

## Goal

Improve trust in delivery promises.

### Promise reliability

- % delivered within communicated ETA
- Mean absolute ETA error
- ETA revision frequency

### Customer friction

- Cancellation rate after delay signal
- Support contacts per 1,000 delayed orders
- Post-delay satisfaction

### Business impact

- Repeat-order rate
- Service-recovery cost per delayed order

### Guardrails

- False-positive alerts
- Notification complaints / opt-outs
- Refund / compensation cost
- Operational workload

## North Star

**% of orders delivered within the communicated ETA**

This keeps the team focused on the quality of the promise rather than rewarding an unrealistically fast initial ETA.
